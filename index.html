Privacy Policy for Olivia Clinic

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Olivia Clinic ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Olivia Clinic mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the App may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information, such as phone number or email address.
  • Account and authentication information.
  • Appointment and service-related information provided by you.
  • Information you voluntarily submit through forms or customer support.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the App, such as device type, operating system, IP address, and application logs.
  • Approximate or precise location information only when a location-based feature requires it and the relevant permission has been granted.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide appointments and other services requested through the App.
  • Communicate with users about appointments, requests, updates, and support.
  • Improve the performance, reliability, and security of the App.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. We may share information only when necessary to provide the requested service, operate the App, protect our rights and users, comply with legal obligations, or work with service providers that process information on our behalf. Such providers are expected to handle information according to applicable privacy and security requirements.

4. Health and Sensitive Information

If the App provides healthcare-related features and you voluntarily submit medical or other sensitive information, we use that information only for the purposes for which it was provided, such as appointment and clinic service management. We apply reasonable safeguards to protect such information.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

If the App provides account creation, users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information through the available in-app account deletion feature or by contacting us using the contact details below. Certain information may be retained where required or permitted by law.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for functions such as hosting, authentication, maps, notifications, analytics, payments, or infrastructure. These services may process limited information as necessary to provide their functions. Their processing is subject to their respective privacy policies.

9. Permissions

The App requests device permissions only when they are needed for a feature that you use. You can manage available permissions through your device settings.
